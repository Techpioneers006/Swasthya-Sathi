PLEASE TAKE CARE

When should you seek emergency help?

Get immediate professional help if any of these serious warning signs occur.

🫁 Severe breathing difficulty
❤️ Severe chest pain
🆘 Unconsciousness or seizure
🩹 Severe bleeding
⚡ Sudden weakness or paralysis
⚠️ Severe allergic reaction
If you believe this is life-threatening, seek emergency medical help immediately.Do not delay professional help to use this website.
